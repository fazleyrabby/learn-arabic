# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Laravel 12/13, PHP 8.4, PostgreSQL, Blade, Alpine.js, Tailwind CSS v4

## Users

Learners seeking a direct, serene, and authentic pathway to understanding Quranic Arabic. Beginners who wish to progress from recognizing individual letters and vowels to understanding vocabulary and analyzing ayaat word-by-word.

## Product Purpose

A minimal, focused web application that bridges foundational Arabic reading with deep Quranic comprehension through structured linguistics and spaced repetition.

## Positioning

Linguistic integrity and distraction-free study over gamified noise. Pure Quranic context without irrelevant conversational vocabulary.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile study environments. Dedicated, contemplative reading, listening, and practice sessions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Complete 28 Arabic letters with 4 contextual forms (isolated, initial, medial, final).
- Harakat module with phonetic rules and audio.
- 100 high-frequency Quranic vocabulary words with root tracking.
- Interactive word-by-word reader for Surah Al-Fatihah with morphology drawer.
- Spaced Repetition (SRS) review engine.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Quranic Arabic  
Aesthetic: Serene, minimal, reverent, distraction-free.

## Product Principles

1. Quran-First: Every word and example maps to authentic Quranic usage.
2. Structured Linguistic Integrity: Authoritative dataset backing (no hallucinated morphology).
3. Serene Craft: Clean typography, generous line-heights, calm color palette.
4. Retention through Recognition: Continuous exposure across script, sound, root, and context.
